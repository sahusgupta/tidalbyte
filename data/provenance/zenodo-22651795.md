## What the primary file is (paper, Table 1)
- One row per **county-hour with a positive processed outage metric and at least one matched NWS VTEC warning**. Hours without outages, or with outages but no warning, are absent. This is a selected sample.
- `max_rolling_avg`: maximum 4-hour trailing-average outage within the clock hour, **in reported customers without power**.
- `median_sum`: county 75th-percentile baseline of positive values over the **whole** record, used by the authors for event segmentation.
- Events (`new_event_no`) are segmented per county. Warnings are attributed if they overlap the event's start-to-peak window: co-occurrence, not causation.
- Missing EAGLE-I source records were zero-filled by the authors. `source_completeness_pct` is the share of the 16 expected 15-minute records present in the trailing 4-hour window.

## Observed facts from our own inspection
- 3,572,394 rows, 3,000+ counties. **Records span 2015-01-02 to 2023-01-03**, despite "2024" in the file name and title.
- Only 30.5% of rows have 100% source-record availability. The median target rises from 0.4 (≤25% availability) to 34.8 customers (100%) in Texas.
- Houston region (13 H-GAC counties): 39,122 rows, 14,559 county outage events. Winter Storm Uri (Feb 2021) dominates (Harris peak 359,049 customers).
